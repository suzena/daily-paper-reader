## 摘要
我们研究了在空间变化流场中对抗性无人水下航行的目的地推理问题。红方航行器被建模为近似沿汉密尔顿-雅可比（HJ）时间最优路径向未知目的地航行，而蓝方航行器观测到该轨迹的带噪声实现。我们推导了该问题的连续时间似然模型，并获得了闭式局部最大似然估计量、恒定记忆多周期估计量以及渐近Cramér-Rao效率结果。Fisher信息由一个组合评分核控制，包含两个加性分量：策略均值敏感性和参考路径敏感性，以及一个乘性漂移敏感性，其主要的几何贡献是当前Hessian矩阵与HJ特征流Jacobi场的缩并。所有三种敏感性均由该Jacobi场诱导，从而建立了流场几何与目的地可识别性之间的可计算联系。我们进一步将该框架扩展到扫掠依赖观测模型，并为蓝方团队制定了主动扫掠设计问题。在涡流和通道剪切流中的数值实验验证了Cramér-Rao预测，并说明了流场几何如何决定哪些目的地可以被可靠推断。

## Abstract
We study destination inference for adversarial unmanned underwater navigation in a spatially varying current field. The red vehicle is modeled as approximately following a Hamilton-Jacobi (HJ) time-optimal path toward an unknown destination, while a blue vehicle observes a noisy realization of that trajectory. We derive a continuous-time likelihood model for this problem and obtain a closed-form local maximum likelihood estimator, a constant-memory multi-period estimator, and an asymptotic Cramér-Rao efficiency result. The Fisher information is governed by a combined score kernel with two additive components, a policy-mean sensitivity and a reference-path sensitivity, and a multiplicative drift sensitivity whose leading geometric contribution is a contraction of the current Hessian with the Jacobi field of the HJ characteristic flow. All three sensitivities are induced by that Jacobi field, yielding a computable link between current-field geometry and destination identifiability. We further extend the framework to a sweep-dependent observation model and formulate an active sweep-design problem for the blue team. Numerical experiments in vortex and channel-shear currents validate the Cramér-Rao prediction and illustrate how current geometry determines which destinations can be reliably inferred.
