## 摘要
海洋垃圾对水下生态系统的威胁日益加剧，这促使人们需要能够在广阔区域内高效定位废弃物的自主勘测方法。现有的勘测方法通常沿预定路径行进，或仅使用单一传感模态，通常是相机（在能见度差时图像质量下降）或声呐（通常噪声大且分辨率低）。我们提出一种主动建图框架，其中前视声呐和相机均输入到共享的贝叶斯占据地图中，并在每一步求解一个优化问题以决定下一个最佳视角。候选视角由两项效用评分，该效用通过体素熵平衡对不确定区域的探索与对可能目标的利用。每个传感器由从数据中确定的、依赖于距离和方位的检测概率与虚警概率表来表征。我们在逼真的水下仿真器中评估该方法，证明主动建图比割草机式覆盖模式更快找到目标，并且双传感器方法优于单独使用任一传感器。

## Abstract
Marine litter is a growing threat to the underwater ecosystem, driving demand for autonomous survey methods that can locate debris efficiently over large areas. Existing survey methods typically follow predefined paths or operate with a single sensing modality, typically a camera (with image quality suffering in poor-visibility conditions) or sonar (usually noisy and low-resolution). We present an active mapping framework in which a forward-looking sonar and a camera both feed into a shared Bayesian occupancy map, and an optimization problem is solved at each step to decide on the next best view. Candidate viewpoints are scored by a two-term utility that balances exploration of uncertain regions via voxel entropy against exploitation of likely objects. Each sensor is characterized by range- and bearing-dependent detection and false-alarm probability tables determined from data. We evaluate the approach in a realistic underwater simulator, demonstrating that active mapping finds objects faster than a lawnmower coverage pattern, and that the dual-sensor approach works better than using either of the individual sensors.
