## 摘要
地理空间基础模型（GeoFMs）为环境监测提供了变革性潜力，但生态学家的采用情况并不均衡。大多数评估以模型为中心，关注架构和基准准确性，却忽视了系统对目标用户是否可用。为弥补这一空白，我们首先开展了一项面向生态与保护科学家的试点专家启发式调查，以识别当前GeoFM开发优先级与其需求之间的错位。基于这些发现并结合HCI理论，我们构建了一个包含七个维度的评估框架，涵盖访问与部署、交互与定制、信任与透明度、社区与支持、科学持久性、多语言支持和离线可用性。随后，两名评估者将该评估框架应用于89个GeoFM。我们发现存在明显的可访问性差距，近三分之一的模型除了源代码外，不向实践者提供任何支持。评分高度一致的维度可作为领域级诊断指标，揭示当前GeoFM的改进空间。

## Abstract
Geospatial foundation models (GeoFMs) offer transformative potential for environmental monitoring, yet adoption among ecologists is uneven. Most evaluations are model-centric, focusing on architecture and benchmark accuracy, which overlooks whether the systems are usable by their intended audiences. To address this gap, we first conducted a pilot expert elicitation survey with ecology and conservation scientists that helped us identify misalignments between current GeoFM development priorities and their needs. Informed by these findings and based on HCI theory, we created a seven-dimension evaluation covering Access & Deployment, Interaction & Customization, Trust & Transparency, Community & Support, Scientific Permanence, Multilingual Support, and Offline Usability. Then, two raters applied this rubric to 89 GeoFMs. We found distinct accessibility gaps where nearly a third provide no support to practitioners beyond their source code. Dimensions along which ratings were highly consistent function as field-level diagnostics, revealing where there is room for improvement for current GeoFMs.
