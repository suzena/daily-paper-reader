## 摘要
海洋物联网（IoT）部署日益依赖空天地海一体化网络（SAGSIN）来连接水下传感器与地面及空间骨干网。然而，这一路径上的异构链路——从带宽受限且高能耗的水声信道到海上高容量光链路——使得海量原始感知数据的传输在时延和能耗两方面代价高昂，并且对于无人值守、电池供电的节点而言难以持续。本文提出一种面向SAGSIN的边缘计算范式，其建立在两个相互耦合的理念之上：多层网络架构（MLNA），用于组织水下、水面、空中以及地面/空间层级；以及多级信息处理（MLIP），在数据沿网络上行时，将其从原始测量逐步精炼为紧凑的事件级表示。我们刻画每一层级的计算、传输和存储能耗，并展示跨层分布式的精炼如何以本地处理代价换取传输与存储开销的节省。随后，我们讨论MLNA-MLIP如何降低时延、增强数据隐私、提升服务可靠性，并通过能量管理延长网络寿命。一项海上监测案例研究量化了由此带来的寿命增益，并确定了最优处理深度。最后概述了开放挑战与未来方向。

## Abstract
Maritime Internet of Things (IoT) deployments increasingly rely on the space-air-ground-sea integrated network (SAGSIN) to connect underwater sensors with terrestrial and space backbones. However, the heterogeneous links along this path, ranging from bandwidth-limited and energy-hungry underwater acoustic channels to high-capacity optical links above the sea, make the transport of massive raw sensing data costly in both latency and energy, and difficult to sustain for unattended, battery-powered nodes. This article presents an edge-computing paradigm for SAGSIN built on two coupled ideas: a Multi-Layer Network Architecture (MLNA) that organizes the underwater, surface, aerial, and ground/space tiers, and Multi-Level Information Processing (MLIP) that progressively refines data from raw measurements toward compact, event-level representations as they ascend the network. We characterize the computation, transmission, and storage energy at each tier and show how distributing refinement across layers trades local processing cost against transmission and storage savings. We then discuss how MLNA-MLIP reduces latency, strengthens data privacy, improves service reliability, and manages energy to prolong network lifetime. A case study on offshore monitoring quantifies the resulting lifetime gains and identifies the optimal processing depth. Open challenges and future directions are outlined.
