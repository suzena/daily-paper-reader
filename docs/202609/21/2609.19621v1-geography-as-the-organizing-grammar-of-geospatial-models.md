## 摘要
地理人工智能（GeoAI）正日益产出可复用的地球表征、物理预测、多模态系统与推理智能体。其进展暴露出两类截然不同的局限。地理完备性追问的是：被表征的世界是否从易于观测的陆地表层与大气场域，延伸至海洋、生物地理、经济、制度与人类能动性。地理智能追问的则是：模型输出是否保留了地点、尺度、关系、过程、不确定性以及有效推断的边界。前者关注模型中存在什么，后者关注可以对其负责任地主张什么。这篇批判性整合综述认为，地理学提供了连接这些维度的组织语法。七项结构性缺口、命题及相应的综述问题，将该论点转化为可检验的要求。由此形成的研究议程提出“活的地理空间模型”（Living Geospatial Models），即通过共享的地理身份、支撑、关系、溯源与不确定性，将地球与人文领域的专业模型耦合起来的联邦式、持续更新系统。其目标并非单一通用网络，而是能够解释联系与变化、预判可能的未来，并支持跨地点、跨尺度的可问责决策的地理空间智能。

## Abstract
GeoAI increasingly produces reusable Earth representation s, physical forecasts, multimodal systems, and reasoning a gents. Their progress exposes two distinct limitations. Geo graphic completeness asks whether the represented world ex tends beyond readily observed land surfaces and atmospher ic fields to oceans, biogeography, economies, institutions, and human agency. Geographic intelligence asks whether model outputs preserve place, scale, relations, process, un certainty, and the limits of valid inference. The first con cerns what exists in a model; the second concerns what may responsibly be claimed about it. This critical integrative review argues that geography provides the organizing gram mar that connects these dimensions. Seven structural gaps, propositions, and corresponding review questions translate the argument into testable requirements. The resulting re search agenda advances Living Geospatial Models as federat ed, continually updated systems that couple specialist Earth and human-domain models through shared geographic iden tity, support, relations, provenance, and uncertainty. The objective is not a single universal network, but geospatial intelligence that can explain connections and change, antic ipate plausible futures, and support accountable decisions across places and scales.
